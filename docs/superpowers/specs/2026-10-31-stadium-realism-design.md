# Stadium journal v4

User-approved direction: prioritize realistic, detailed stadium models; pointer-responsive interactive cards; matching home-team anthems or chants as quiet, fading pure audio, with a small mute control.

## Presentation

Replace the image-plus-information-box cards with immersive photographic covers. Two columns on desktop and one on phones. Keep the stadium name and home club visible; reveal the story excerpt and entry affordance on hover or keyboard focus. Pointer position drives restrained tilt, image parallax, and a luminous highlight. Touch targets remain explicit and reduced-motion preferences disable movement.

## Stadium exhibit

Curate publicly embeddable authored models by stadium identity and visual quality. Preserve the original host and attribution; do not extract restricted model files. Default to an authored detailed model where available and usable. Retain a detailed local architectural reconstruction and real photographs as alternatives. Distinguish reconstruction from surveyed or authored assets in the captions. Maintain drag, pan, and zoom. Only one model viewer may run at a time; switching or closing releases it.

Improve local reconstruction with individual chair backs and seats, sector aisles, barriers, concourses, roof trusses, façade divisions, goal nets, realistic turf, a ground plaza, and directional shadows. Add exterior and inner-bowl camera presets.

## Home sound

Replace original procedural music with verified matching team anthems or crowd chants published as actual audio by clubs or supporter sources. Each source carries its track name and source link. Start in the stadium-opening click handler; fade from zero to 10% over about 3.5 seconds. A small persistent mute button remains the only playback control. Suspend on page hiding and dispose on closing or changing stadium. Do not use video, hidden video players, commercial previews restricted to promotion, unrelated songs, or synthesized substitutes. If a reliable source is unavailable or fails, show an honest quiet status.
